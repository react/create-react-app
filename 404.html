Why rent from us

Straight answers, and a vehicle that turns up

Renting a vehicle should not be the complicated part of a trip. We tell you the rate, the limits and the deposit before you take the car, and we bring it to you wherever you are in the district.

Every vehicle carries three numbers before you take it: the daily rate, the kilometres included each day, and what anything beyond that costs. The deposit is stated too, and it comes back when the car does. Nothing is discovered at the end.

We work across the whole of Kanyakumari district — Nagercoil, Marthandam, Colachel, Thuckalay and the towns between. Tell us where you are and we will sort out getting the vehicle to you, including the airport if you are flying in.

A self drive car for the week, a bike for running about, a decorated car for the wedding, and a driver for the day everyone goes sightseeing. Booked together, with one person to call rather than four.

Booking, start to finish

How it works

  1. 01

    Pick your car

    Browse the fleet and choose what suits the trip — a hatchback for the city, an SUV for the highway.

  2. 02

    Send an enquiry

    Tell us your dates. We'll call back to confirm the car is free and agree the rate.

  3. 03

    Collect and drive

    Bring a valid licence and ID. Pay the deposit, take the keys, and the car is yours.

  4. 04

    Drop it back at the time agreed. We check it over, and the deposit comes back the same day.

Deposits are refundable. It is returned after the car comes back, less any extra-KM charges or damage. KM limits and extra-KM rates are listed on every car above, so there is nothing to discover later.

Where people go

The coast, the temples and the waterfalls are all an easy drive from Nagercoil. Take the car and go at your own pace.

Coast

Kanyakumari and the Vivekananda Rock

The southern tip, where the sea on three sides meets at one point. Sunrise is what people come for; the ferry out to the Vivekananda Rock and the Thiruvalluvar statue queues in season, so go early or accept it will take an hour.

Coast

Vattakottai Fort

A small granite fort right on the shore about six kilometres up the coast from Kanyakumari, with the sea on one side and the hills behind. Half an hour is enough, and the drive there along the coast road is half the reason to go.

Directionsto Vattakottai Fort, opens Google Maps in a new tab
Heritage

Heritage

Padmanabhapuram Palace

The old wooden palace of the Travancore kings at Thuckalay, and the largest of its kind anywhere. Bigger inside than it looks and worth ninety minutes. It shuts earlier in the afternoon than most people expect, so check before setting off.

Directionsto Padmanabhapuram Palace, opens Google Maps in a new tab
Temple

Temple

Thanumalayan Temple, Suchindram

A short run out of Nagercoil to one of the district's great temples, known for its musical pillars and the tall Hanuman statue. Like most temples here it closes in the middle of the day and opens again late afternoon.

Directionsto Thanumalayan Temple, Suchindram, opens Google Maps in a new tab
Falls

Falls

Thirparappu Falls

A broad, low waterfall on the Kodayar river with a bathing area below it, inland towards the hills. Fullest after the monsoon and quiet on a weekday. The road out is tarred with some broken stretches — unhurried is the right pace.

Directionsto Thirparappu Falls, opens Google Maps in a new tab
Engineering

Engineering

Mathur Trough Aqueduct

A working aqueduct carrying water across the Pahrali valley on concrete piers, high enough that walking its length is the point. Usually combined with Thirparappu, which is a few kilometres away.

Directionsto Mathur Trough Aqueduct, opens Google Maps in a new tab
Heritage

Heritage

Chitharal Jain Monuments

Rock-cut Jain carvings from the ninth century on a hill near Marthandam, reached by a short climb. Quiet, rarely crowded, and the view from the top is worth the walk on its own.

Directionsto Chitharal Jain Monuments, opens Google Maps in a new tab
Coast

Coast

Muttom Beach and Lighthouse

Rocky shore rather than sand, with a lighthouse above it and far fewer people than Kanyakumari. Good at sunset. The rocks are slippery where the sea reaches them, which is worth saying to anyone with children.

Directionsto Muttom Beach and Lighthouse, opens Google Maps in a new tab
Coast

Coast

Sanguthurai Beach

The closest proper beach to Nagercoil, with a long stretch of sand and a footbridge out over the water. Quiet on weekday mornings and busy at weekends. Twenty minutes from town, which makes it the easy evening drive.

Directionsto Sanguthurai Beach, opens Google Maps in a new tab
Hills

Hills

Pechiparai Dam

A reservoir in the hills north-west of Nagercoil, surrounded by rubber and forest. The drive is the attraction as much as the dam — this is the greenest part of the district and the road climbs gently the whole way.

Directionsto Pechiparai Dam, opens Google Maps in a new tab

Hills

Maruthuvazhmalai

A hill of medicinal herbs just outside Nagercoil with a temple at the top and a steep set of steps to reach it. Early morning is the sensible time; there is no shade on the climb.

Olakaruvi Falls

A falls in the Western Ghats reached by a proper walk uphill from where the road ends, which keeps the crowds away. For anyone who wants a half day on foot rather than a drive between car parks.

Questions we are asked most

If yours is not here, call or send a WhatsApp message and we will answer it straight.

Do I need a deposit to take a self drive car?

Yes, a refundable security deposit, and you get it back when the vehicle comes back in the same condition. The amount depends on the vehicle and we tell you the figure before you book, not when you arrive.

What documents do I need to rent a car without a driver?

Your original driving licence and one photo ID. We take a copy at handover. For visitors from abroad, bring your passport and an international driving permit alongside your home licence.

Is there a kilometre limit, and what happens if I go over it?

Every vehicle comes with a daily kilometre allowance, and anything beyond it is charged at a fixed rate per kilometre. Both numbers are on the tariff page and on your booking, so there is nothing to work out afterwards.

Can you deliver the car to Nagercoil railway station or the airport?

We deliver and collect across Kanyakumari district — Nagercoil, Kanyakumari, Marthandam, Colachel, Thuckalay and the towns around them — including the railway station and the bus stand. Tell us where you land and when, and we will say what we can do.

Can I take the car into Kerala?

Usually yes — Trivandrum is an hour away and a lot of our rentals cross the border. Tell us where you are going when you book, so the paperwork is right before you set off.

Do you rent two-wheelers as well as cars?

Yes. Scooters and motorcycles by the hour, the day or the week, on the same terms as the cars. A two-wheeler is the easier way around Nagercoil and out to the coast.
